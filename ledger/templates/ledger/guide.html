How HydrantProof works

Five jobs, one refusal, and the exact vocabulary every screen uses. Everything on this page is read from the same constants the engine runs on — it is a reference, not a description that can drift.

The five jobs

1 · Registry. Every hydrant with its attributes, its provenance and a three-state service lifecycle, plus an append-only status log that names who changed it and why. Open the registry.

2 · Ledger. NFPA 291 flow tests: static, residual, per-outlet pitot readings, an optional meter total. The server computes the discharge and the rated flow at {{ target_residual_psi|floatformat:0 }} psi residual, stamps the formula version, and keeps full history with supersession-based corrections.

3 · Inspections and deficiencies. Append-only inspections on their own recency window. A FAIL opens one gating row per code; a later OK closes every open row transactionally.

4 · Readiness. The three states below, plus information-level attention flags, ranked overdue lists, out-of-service expiry with acknowledgement, and a notification outbox.

5 · Water-supply worksheet. For each protected parcel, every hydrant inside {{ credit_radius_ft|floatformat:0 }} ft with its credited contribution or its explicit exclusion reason, and one click to an evidence pack. Open the worksheet.

The one refusal

HydrantProof does not predict a Public Protection Classification and does not compute FSRS point values. ISO (Verisk) assigns classifications under a licensed, copyrighted schedule this product cannot quote or encode.

What the district gets instead: complete worksheet inputs, ranked gaps, and a neutral readiness profile organised the way the public FSRS water-supply section is structured. No points are computed anywhere in this product.

Proof posture — stated plainly

HydrantProof's proof is proof of records — attributed, append-only, versioned, reproducible — not proof of physical presence. The capture page records browser geolocation with its accuracy, a client capture timestamp and photo metadata, but nothing in this version attests that a person stood at the hydrant.

Queued field submissions are idempotent by client_uuid, not cryptographically signed. Every artifact is labelled: {{ artifact_label }}
