Constants this build pins

code, not policy

The NFPA 291 arithmetic is fixed in code: discharge constant 29.83, target residual 20 psi, drop exponent 0.54, formula version nfpa291-pct-drop-v1. The coefficient presets, the extrapolation ceiling, the trend threshold, the distribution bands and the flow-band proxy are provisional and verification-gated — every pack prints the provisional banner, and the district is told to verify them against a licensed NFPA 291 edition before submitting to a surveyor.
